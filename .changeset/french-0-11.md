Translates everything Goodfellow 0.11 adds to the admin panel, 53 strings in all: publishing new pages, which now start out not published, updating installed blocks, the AI assistant's free services and its reports, Site Settings' Free services list, the editor's drag handle, and the page settings' new groups. Three strings whose English changed are translated again, and the Item field block, Entry field's new name, keeps its French. Strings whose English only gained a comma before "and" or "or" keep their French, which doesn't use one.
